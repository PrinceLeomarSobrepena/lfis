The workflow

One path from found to returned.

Three stages, the same for every site, every item, every team member — so nothing depends on memory or a spreadsheet.

01

Report

Staff or guests log a found or lost item in under a minute — photo, location, and description, from a desk or a phone.

02

Match

The matching engine cross-references descriptions, locations, and time windows, and surfaces likely matches automatically.

03

Return

The owner is notified, verifies ownership, and picks up or ships the item — with every step logged for audit.

The platform

Built for teams running more than one site.

Everything a lost and found program needs to run consistently, from intake to disposition.

Multi-site intake

Front desk, security post, kiosk, or mobile — every item enters the same queue, tagged to its site.

Automatic matching

Found items and lost reports are compared continuously, ranked by likelihood, and queued for staff review.

Chain-of-custody log

Every handoff, storage move, and status change is timestamped and attributed — ready for an audit at any time.

Guest self-service

Owners search and file a claim themselves, cutting the calls and emails your front desk has to field.

Storage & disposition

Shelf and bin locations, hold periods, and disposal or donation workflows, tracked against policy.

Return rate, average time to return, and volume by site and category, in one dashboard.
